## Core principles

- 每场面试都有 **三个关键时刻**：开头、结束、以及被拒之后。你会影响结果，取决于你知道在这些时刻说什么。
- 面试是一种 **相互探索（mutual discovery）** 的过程，你可以向面试官 **索要更多信息**：他们最关心的痛点和需求是什么。
- 开头用一行话 **改变权力动态**：从 JD 列出 X/Y 重要点，然后反问他们还最看重什么。
- 结束用 **recency effect**（首尾效应）给一个“粘住”的总结，让他们记得最后一句。
- 被拒后别崩：用 HR/邮件 **优雅地保持关系**，例如在三个月后再联系。

## Interview philosophy

顶尖候选人不是把面试当作单向展示，而是把它当作“你帮我更清楚我是否能解决你的问题、我帮你更清楚你是否会雇我”的过程；因此影响力来自三个关键时段的策略动作。

## Evaluation criteria

- 开头是否把话语权从“自我介绍”转为“向对方确认最在乎什么”
- 结束是否主动总结对方优先级（X/Y）并表达你如何帮到他们
- 被拒邮件是否保持尊重、表达兴趣、并提出未来联系（door cracked open）

## Common candidate mistakes

- 还没搞清对方最痛点就直接把一套自我介绍倒出来
- 面试结束只说“thanks”然后走人，错过 **最后被记住** 的窗口
- 被拒后完全消失，错过“通常 just means not now”的长期机会

## Follow-up questions

- 开头我有没有问：除了 X/Y 之外你还最关心哪些技能/经历？
- 结束时我有没有做一个基于对方优先级的 assertive wrap-up？
- 被拒后我有没有写：感谢 + 仍然很感兴趣 + 未来三个月再 check-in？

## Answer frameworks

### Three key moments

1. **Beginning（开头）**：在自我介绍前确认对方最在乎的技能/经历。
2. **End（结束）**：用 recency 让最后的总结“粘住”。
3. **Rejection（被拒）**：优雅保持门缝，三个月后再联系。

### 开头一句话（verbatim）

- “Sure, before I dive in, I see from the job description that X and Y are important in this role. Are there any other skills or experiences you'd be most interested in?”

## Good vs bad examples

### 开头：自爆式自我介绍 vs 合作式反问

**Bad（常见做法）**：还没问清需求就先进入“relevant self intro script”。

**Good（verbatim）**：

> Sure, before I dive in, I see from the job description that X and Y are important in this role. Are there any other skills or experiences you'd be most interested in? I have broad experience and I want to highlight what's most relevant to you. This one line shifts the entire power dynamic.

### Beginning — “shifts the entire power dynamic”

- **Central question this Short addresses:** 面试开头如何用一句话影响结果
- **Interview question(s) this sample answers:** 自我介绍前的确认提问（可用于开头）
- **Jonathan's sample answer (verbatim):**

> Sure, before I dive in, I see from the job description that X and Y are important in this role. Are there any other skills or experiences you'd be most interested in? I have broad experience and I want to highlight what's most relevant to you. This one line shifts the entire power dynamic.

### End — recency effect close

- **Central question this Short addresses:** 面试结束怎么说，才能被记住
- **Interview question(s) this sample answers:** 结束收口（wrap up）
- **Jonathan's sample answer (verbatim):**

> You could say, thanks for sharing your top priorities for this role such as X and Y. To summarize, I really think my skills and a an experience in B can make an impact. I'm excited about how I can help. This closes the loop assertively and gives a strong final impression.

### Rejection — keep the door cracked open

- **Central question this Short addresses:** 如何优雅回复被拒邮件
- **Interview question(s) this sample answers:** 被拒后的 HR follow-up
- **Jonathan's sample answer (verbatim):**

> Hi, name. Thank you for getting back to me. While I'm disappointed, I completely understand. I really enjoyed our conversations and I remain very interested in company. Would you be open to me checking in again in three months to see if anything has opened up? I'd love to reconnect if there's a future fit. Thank you again, your name.

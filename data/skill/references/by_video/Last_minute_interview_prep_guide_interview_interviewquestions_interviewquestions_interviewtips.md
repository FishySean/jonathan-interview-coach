## Core principles

- Coming up soon: **more notes won’t save you — focus will.** One-hour checklist beats static brain.
- **10 min company:** one-sentence what they do + how they make money; careers page → one value for “why here”; LinkedIn interviewer detail for small talk/Q&A.
- **JD with AI:** don’t skim — ask “If I were them, what kind of person would I want?” (he has a GPT prompt to steal; not spoken in full in transcript).
- **Script answers:** memorable intro + why here; for behavioral, **three career stories** matching skills from JD analysis.
- Like finals: don’t read whole textbook — chapters most likely to show up. One hour → ahead of 80%; more time next round to beat 100%.

## Interview philosophy

Last-hour prep is prioritization: business clarity, value hook, interviewer humanization, JD-derived persona, and three proof stories.

## Evaluation criteria

- Kid-simple business + monetization sentence
- Value named in why-here
- Interviewer-specific icebreaker
- JD → desired persona → three matching stories
- Scripted intro / why here

## Common candidate mistakes

- More notes, less focus
- Skimming JD without “who would I hire?” lens
- No three stories mapped to JD skills
- Generic Q lists only

## Follow-up questions

- “One sentence: what do they do and how make money?”
- “Which value + three JD-skill stories?”
- “What LinkedIn detail for small talk?”

## Answer frameworks

### One-hour checklist

1. Company (10 min): model, value, interviewer  
2. JD + AI persona analysis  
3. Script intro / why here + pick 3 behavioral stories

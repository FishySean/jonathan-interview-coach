## Core principles

- They’re not asking for your dreams — hiring is expensive; they want to know **will you leave before they profit?**
- Answer must show you’re not just interested — **you’re invested.**
- Show a **real connection** (product, values, someone there) — not generic “I like your culture.”
- Best: **specific long-term plan with them.**
- They’re asking if you’re worth the risk — tell them what they’re dying to hear.

## Interview philosophy

“Why here?” is a retention / ROI risk question. Specificity + long-horizon career fit beats flattery.

## Evaluation criteria

- Real connection (product/values/person)
- Specific (not “I like culture”)
- Long-term plan with the company
- Invested, not merely interested

## Common candidate mistakes

- “I like your company / culture” (heard before; not believed)
- Talking about personal dreams without investment signal
- No specificity, no long-term plan

## Follow-up questions

- “What product/value/person is my real connection?”
- “What’s my long-term skill goal that only this place accelerates?”
- “Does my answer reduce their ‘will you leave early?’ risk?”

## Answer frameworks

1. Real connection (specific)  
2. Long-term career goal that maps to what they’re famous for  
3. Value resonance (e.g. done over perfect)  
4. Close: long impactful career with you

## Core principles

- Right after the interview, **read the interviewer’s last words** for signal.
- **Neutral:** “We’ll get back to you by Friday” — more candidates coming; don’t over-read.
- **Good:** “When can you start?” / “Do you have other offers?” — top candidate; lock-down interest. Answer truthfully but tactically as in-demand.
- **Bad:** Vague next steps / disengaged Q&A — “we’ll let you know,” “couple of weeks,” brief answers, not selling the role.
- Regardless of signal, always do the same three things: thank-you email, reflect/summarize improvements, move on (“thank you, next”).

## Interview philosophy

Closing language is a cheap readout of priority. Post-interview process stays constant so you don’t spiral on ambiguous neutrals or bad signs.

## Evaluation criteria

| Closing cue | Read |
|-------------|------|
| Back by [day] after more interviews | Neutral / unsure |
| Start date / other offers | Strong interest |
| Vague timeline / brief Q&A / no sell | Likely not moving forward |

## Common candidate mistakes

- Assuming Friday callback = doom or win
- Oversharing other interviews without positioning as top choice
- Skipping thank-you / reflection / next opportunity after any outcome

## Follow-up questions

- “What were their exact last words about next steps?”
- “Did they sell the role in Q&A or disengage?”
- “Did I send thanks, note improvements, and move on?”

## Answer frameworks

### When asked about other interviews (good-sign scenario)

Be strategically vague + name them as top choice because XYZ.

### Always-do trio

1. Thank-you email (enthusiasm, professional)  
2. Reflect / summarize improvement areas  
3. Thank you, next — more opportunities await  

## Good vs bad examples

### Neutral (verbatim)

> we'll get back to you by Friday… Overall, this is a neutral sign because this is what they will say to most candidates that they aren't sure about yet. Don't assume anything else here.

### Good (verbatim)

> when can you start? Or do you have other offers right now? That is a good sign… be truthful, but an attackful way that positions you as an in-demand candidate… I'm in the early stages of my search and I'm pursuing a few promising opportunities. But your company is my top choice because of XYZ.

*(ASR: “attackful” ≈ tactful.)*

### Bad (verbatim)

> we'll let you know or we'll get back to you in a couple of weeks or so are intentionally non-committal… interviewer is really brief in their answers and don't seem to be trying to sell the role

## Core principles

- Work does **not** speak for itself — managers of large teams only see bits and pieces.
- Most of what managers “know” comes from what people **tell them** + evidence.
- Goal of reviews: **show impact** by selecting the right evidence — not passive judgment, not exaggeration.
- Tool: the **brag book**. Western workplace ≠ humble Olympics (谦虚) — humble gets forgotten.
- You can’t get rewarded for what your manager doesn’t know — **brag as if your career depended on it**.

## Interview philosophy

(Manager reviews / self-evaluation.) Managers agree more easily when you bring the right facts. Development areas: don’t minimize weaknesses — **maximize effort** working on them (“bring solutions, not problems”).

## Evaluation criteria

Brag-book quality:

- One story per project (STAR), factual/objective
- Actions linked to company values + personal development goals
- Business impact: how company makes money → how your work contributes
- Receipts: screenshots, reports, code, messages
- Weaknesses paired with concrete improvement actions

## Common candidate mistakes

- Believing work speaks for itself.
- Passive waiting for judgment.
- Humble-Olympics invisibility.
- Minimizing weaknesses instead of showing improvement effort.
- No receipts / no money-impact link.

## Follow-up questions

Self-check while building the book:

- “How does my company make money?”
- “How does my work contribute to that?”

On development areas: show solution, not only the problem.

## Answer frameworks

### Brag book build

1. One STAR story per project (factual).
2. Connect to company values + development goals.
3. Show business impact (money/contribution).
4. Attach receipts.
5. Weaknesses: honest + maximized effort / solution.

### Weakness / development template

Name the area → what you’ve started doing to fix it.

## Good vs bad examples

### Scenario: Development areas

**Bad:** Minimize weaknesses.

**Good:**

> stakeholder communication is one area that I'm developing. I've started daily updates and teams to keep alignment.

### Scenario: Humility

**Bad:** Stay 谦虚 / compete in humble Olympics → forgotten.

**Good:** Build and use a brag book with evidence.

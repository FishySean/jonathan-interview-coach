## Core principles

- **Change one thing: stop using STAR** for standing out — STAR is a neat *report* interviewers like for clarity; **reports aren’t memorable; stories are.**
- Interviewers teach STAR because it’s easy to parse — good for them, not always for you.
- Use **HEALER:** Hook → Emergency → Approach → Launch → Effect → Reflection.
- **Offers don’t go to the best candidates — they go to the best storytellers.** Stop reporting on your career; start selling it.

## Interview philosophy

Behavioral answers should create emotional memory and distinct thinking, not event logs. HEALER forces stakes, unique thinking, concrete action, impact, and role-fit learning.

## Evaluation criteria

HEALER checklist:

| Beat | Job |
|------|-----|
| **Hook** | Line that grabs attention |
| **Emergency** | Problem + why critical |
| **Approach** | Unique thinking vs other candidates |
| **Launch** | Specific concrete solve details |
| **Effect** | Impact on team/company |
| **Reflection** | What you learned → better fit for this role |

## Common candidate mistakes

- Obeying STAR because “even interviewers tell me to” without asking why
- Delivering forgettable neat reports
- No hook/stakes/unique approach/reflection

## Follow-up questions

- “What’s my hook line?”
- “What’s the emergency and why critical?”
- “What’s my unique approach vs other candidates?”
- “What concrete launch details prove I did it?”
- “Effect on team/company? Reflection tied to this role?”

## Jonathan's sample answers (verbatim)

### HEALER personal story (coaching origin)

- **Central question this Short addresses:** Tell better interview stories immediately — behavioral hack beyond STAR.
- **Interview question(s) this sample answers:** Behavioral “tell me about a time…” / career-change / why coaching — demo of HEALER.
- **Jonathan's sample answer (verbatim):**

> On a summer night in 2023, just as we were turning off the lights, my wife looked at me and asked, why are we here? What are we doing? Minutes later, I realized she was having a stroke. At 30 years old, she was fighting for her life in emergency. Her fragility and my own hit me like a thunderbolt. Before that, my plan was to play it safe, you know, build coaching on the side, keep my salary, wait for a sign to go all in. I thought I was playing it safe, but seeing my wife in emergency made me realize that waiting for perfect timing is just waiting for death because life is movement. And when you stop moving out of fear, you stop living. So I moved. I quit my job. I built Mr. Jonathan office full time. I stopped waiting and I started living. Since then over 100,000 Chinese professionals follow my work online. I've helped hundreds to land jobs and promotions in Western workplaces. I've helped them to stop waiting and to start moving too. I wish I could say that it felt perfect. It didn't. It felt terrifying, but it was also the most alive that I felt in years. And it taught me how we spend our days is of course how we spend our lives

## Core principles

- **One sentence separates you:** “Most people do X, I do Y, because Z.”
- Prep from the JD: for each key requirement (leadership, communication, problem solving…), summarize **one clear approach/philosophy** you’ve developed.
- That sentence signals you’ve done it so many times you’ve **summarized your own approach**.
- After the knockout sentence, add a **short story** proving the approach — don’t stop at claiming skill.

## Interview philosophy

On-spot rambling and generic answers (“I handle conflict by compromising” — same as 5/10 candidates) fail influence/conflict questions. Pre-baked philosophies + Most people X / I do Y / because Z make you unforgettable; proof story seals it.

## Evaluation criteria

- Differentiated approach vs “most people”
- Clear because-rationale
- Brief past proof after the sentence
- Mapped to JD key requirements

## Common candidate mistakes

- Scrambling into word salad on conflict / influence-without-authority
- Generic compromise-style answers shared with half the slate
- Saying you know how without proving with a short story

## Follow-up questions

- “For each JD skill, what’s my one-sentence philosophy?”
- “What’s my Most people X / I do Y / because Z?”
- “What’s the short proof story after that sentence?”

## Answer frameworks

1. JD → key skills → one approach each (borrow his list if stuck)
2. Answer formula: **Most people do X, I do Y, because Z**
3. Add short demonstration story from past work

## Good vs bad examples

### Conflict — generic vs philosophy sentence

**Scenario:** “How would you handle conflict?” / influence without authority.

**Bad (verbatim):**

> i handle conflict by compromising basically the same thing that five out of 10 other candidates before you have said

**Good (verbatim):**

> most people try to resolve conflicts by compromising but i start by identifying and aligning on our shared goals from the start because once we realize that we're on the same side well conflict turns us from enemies into partners

**Then (verbatim):**

> after that knockout sentence just add a short story demonstrating your approach from your past work experience now you aren't just saying hey i know how to do this you're proving it

## Core principles

- “How are you?” 通常是你面试里的第一个问题：虽然它看起来 casual，但面试官还是在“从起点就评估你”。
- 他给的是一个 **唯一模板**：把聊天变成第一印象的优势。
- 模板核心：做作但有礼貌的兴奋（doing great + thanks），再加一两句个性化的观察（painting / artwork）和对方近期新闻话题（banana phone 16）。

## Interview philosophy

把 “how are you” 当成你展示准备度与关系建立能力的入口：你不是在回答，而是在拉开一段顺畅、专业、带细节的交流。

## Evaluation criteria

- 开头状态是否积极（I'm doing great）
- 是否表达感谢与对时间的理解（busy time of year… grateful for your time）
- 是否有个性化观察与相关新闻点（painting background / banana phone 16）
- 是否以自信的继续对话收尾（Now let's go）

## Common candidate mistakes

- 回答太空洞：fine / good / same as usual
- 不准备任何个性化细节
- 只说自己，不回应对方背景和信息

## Follow-up questions

- 我有没有准备一句“why grateful / busy time of year”？
- 我有没有准备一个能从对方背景或新闻引出来的观察点？

## Answer frameworks

### “How are you?” single-template answer

直接套用模板，替换：你的视频/背景里看到的点 + 近期新闻信息 + 你自己的背景适配句。

## Good vs bad examples

### 不准备 vs 用模板

**Bad（典型）**：回答“fine, good, thanks”但没有后续细节。

**Good（verbatim）**：模板开头与细节段落见 section 8。

## Jonathan's sample answers (verbatim)

### Full “How are you?” template（verbatim）

- **Central question this Short addresses:** How are you in interviews (first question template)
- **Interview question(s) this sample answers:** “How are you?”
- **Jonathan's sample answer (verbatim):**

> Another day in paradise. I just came back from a morning jog, which I've recently been trying out as a replacement for my usual cup of coffee. So far it's working. I think I might just kick my caffeine habit. How are you? I'm doing great. Thanks for asking. I'm excited to chat with you today, and I'm particularly grateful for your time, as I figure it's a particularly busy time of year for you and the team. I've got to say, I'm excited for this. I've been a loyal client for many years, and it's a real treat to meet you and discuss how I might become a member of the team one day. Couldn't be better. By the way, I love that painting in your background. I've been looking for some artwork from my office as well. I'm great. I was thrilled to read about your launch of the banana phone 16 in the news lately. I think you and your team must be so thrilled and maybe a bit exhausted. Here's a cheat sheet that you can screenshot and save for your next interview. Now let's go.

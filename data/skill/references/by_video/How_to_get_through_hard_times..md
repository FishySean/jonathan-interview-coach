## Core principles

- Hard times (tariffs, visa, layoffs, AI) — planning harder / working twice as hard still leaves anxiety; maybe the model is wrong.
- You don’t **survive** tough times — you **flow** through them.
- You can do everything right (skills, hard work, good person) and still get terrible outcomes — **we are not in control**.
- First shift: **perspective** — flow into chaos instead of resisting it.
- Second: control **reactions** — don’t only shrink inward / avoid all risk.
- Question isn’t “should I take risks?” — it’s **which risks are worth taking**: smart risks (unlimited upside, capped downside) vs stupid risks (ruin to “make it back”).
- Immigrant/Chinese diaspora frame: crossed oceans, new languages, rebuilt from zero — **flow is in our blood**.
- Wish: not survival — **flow**.

## Interview philosophy

(Career anxiety / layoff / market chaos coaching.) Control freaks over-plan every next step; flow people control relationship to money/outcomes and pick asymmetric bets (skills, small business) over revenge trading.

## Evaluation criteria

- Perspective: controllable vs not (markets vs relationship with money/needs)
- Reaction: shrink vs selective smart risk
- Risk quality: capped downside / open upside vs ruinous recovery bets

## Common candidate mistakes

- Only plan/work harder → still dread the unknown.
- Control-freak narration: “lose everything / no mistakes.”
- Shrinking inward; treating all risk as unsafe.
- Stupid risk after losses (e.g. loan → all-in Bitcoin to make money back fast).

## Follow-up questions

Coach:

- “What can you actually control here?”
- “Are you resisting chaos or flowing with a perspective shift?”
- “Is this a smart risk or a stupid risk?”

## Answer frameworks

### Control freak vs flow (market crash)

**Control freak:** terrified of losing everything; must plan next step carefully, no mistakes.

**Flow:** can’t control markets; can control relationship with money — do I need another Labubu to be peaceful/healthy/happy?

### Smart vs stupid risk (same crash)

**Stupid:** “Losing so much — loan + all into Bitcoin to make it back.”

**Smart:** Invest in yourself — small business, learn a key skill/course; unlimited upside; worst case lose small investment.

## Jonathan's sample answers (verbatim)

### Flow thesis + risk scripts

- **Central question this Short addresses:** How to get through hard times — flow, perspective, smart vs stupid risk.
- **Interview question(s) this sample answers:** Career anxiety / layoff / uncertainty coaching (not a classic interview Q).

> You don't survive tough times. You flow through them.

> You can do everything right. Build skills, work hard, be a good person. And still, terrible stuff happens to you.

I'm going to invest in myself, start a small business, learn a key new skill and a course. These risks offer unlimited upside, and in the worst case, I lose my small investment.

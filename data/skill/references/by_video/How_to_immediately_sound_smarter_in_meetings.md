## Core principles

- 让你在会议里显得更聪明/更有自信的关键：**你说话的策略与用词结构**。
- 这条 Short 给的是“**五句你应该跳过**”和“五句更聪明替代”的对照：避免尴尬/被忽略。
- 常见“尴尬”来源包括：频繁道歉、让别人重复、用太多不确定语气、拖延把决定说清楚。
- 更聪明的方式：用简洁、明确的请求与会议管理动作（clarify / take offline / offer viewpoint / suggest revisiting and achieving goals）。

## Interview philosophy

会议里展现专业的方式不是“表达更多”，而是用清晰的话语结构让团队快速达成理解与决策。

## Evaluation criteria

- 你是否能用更直接的语言让对方清楚你在做什么（理解确认/离线跟进/给观点/把讨论拉回主题）
- 你是否能减少低价值的拖延性表达（例如反复“sorry / repeat / could you repeat”）

## Common candidate mistakes

- “Sorry + repeat that please” 让你显得不在状态
- 多次请求对方澄清但不推进结论
- 讨论跑题时不做收敛（off-topic 不引导回目标）

## Follow-up questions

- 我在会议里是用“提问确认”还是“推进决策”？
- 我是否用一句话把讨论从跑题拉回主题并强调会议目标？

## Answer frameworks

### Smarter-meeting phrase moves（抓住这几类动作）

1. **理解确认**（ensure I fully understand）
2. **离线跟进**（take this offline + follow up individually）
3. **表达分歧**（I disagree and here's why + acknowledge perspective）
4. **替代观点**（offer another viewpoint）
5. **收敛回主题**（suggest revisit topic A to achieve goals）

## Good vs bad examples

### 跳过 vs 更聪明的说法（verbatim）

**Bad / skip（verbatim）**：

> Sorry, I was multitasking. Could you repeat that please?

> Could you clarify your point for me?

> I want to make sure I fully understand.

**Good / smarter（verbatim）**：

> Let's take this offline. I'll follow up individually after the meeting to explore this in more detail.

> I'm sorry, but I disagree and here's why.

> I think we're going off topic.

> I suggest that we revisit topic A so that we can achieve our goals for this meeting before coming back to this.

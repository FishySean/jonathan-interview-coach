## Core principles

- 在北美求职里，`hard worker / fast learner / team player` 这类表述太泛，难以拿 offer。
- 不是这些品质不重要，而是它们没有翻译成西方 hiring manager 真正评估的价值信号。
- 三组“翻译”：
  1. **team player → influence**
  2. **fast learner → resourcefulness**
  3. **hard worker → results driven**
- 本质：把“可被默认的好人特质”变成“可证明的业务影响与决策能力”。

## Interview philosophy

面试不是道德评选，而是价值评估。你需要把文化里“努力/配合”的美德，翻译成“可衡量结果、资源整合、推动决策”的语言。

## Evaluation criteria

- 是否从抽象品质切换到可验证行为和结果
- 是否有具体场景（cross-functional alignment、VBA macro、under budget delivery）
- 是否把语义从“我很乖”转成“我能影响结果”

## Common candidate mistakes

- 只报标签词（hard worker, fast learner, team player）
- 把“努力”当成果
- 缺少业务影响量化和决策影响描述

## Follow-up questions

- 我目前有哪些“标签词”可以翻译成 outcome 语言？
- 我是否给了可验证的具体例子（时间、影响、节省、提前交付）？

## Answer frameworks

### Phrase translation matrix

1. Team player -> “I lead with influence” + alignment example
2. Fast learner -> “I’m highly resourceful” + self-initiated upskilling example
3. Hard worker -> “I’m results driven” + high-impact prioritization outcome

## Good vs bad examples

### Team player -> influence（verbatim）

> instead of saying, I work well with others, you can say, I lead with influence.

> I led a cross functional meeting where I helped two teams align on a conflicting timeline, saving the project from delay.

### Fast learner -> resourceful（verbatim）

> instead of saying, I can learn quickly, say, I'm highly resourceful.

> I binge watched Excel VBA tutorials over one weekend and built a macro that saved us five hours per week.

### Hard worker -> results driven（verbatim）

> effort without results is not enough.

> say, I'm results driven. I prioritized high impact tasks and love the team to deliver two weeks early under budget.

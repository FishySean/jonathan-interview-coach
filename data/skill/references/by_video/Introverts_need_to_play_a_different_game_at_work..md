## Core principles

- Workplace often rewards loud/first speakers — introverts feel they’re playing an extrovert game they weren’t built for.
- Lie: must always be sociable; alone = antisocial. Forcing 24/7 connection drains introverts.
- **Return to the water:** recharge in natural habitat (e.g. 30 min reflect after back-to-back meetings).
- Protect space: ask for **TLDR / async** instead of surprise “hop on a call.”
- Events: be seen, talk to who you want, then **unapologetic French exit** (no goodbyes when it’s time).
- Meetings: **don’t need to be loud to lead** — depth, observation, deep questions, intentional listening = superpower.
- Own it: “I’m an introvert — if I disappear early, it’s not you, it’s me.” Permission to exist as you are.
- You’re not a broken bird — a fish out of water; return to your ocean to breathe.

## Interview philosophy

Credibility isn’t volume. Introvert advantage is careful words that quiet the room. Same identity honesty helps interviews and workplace presence without fake extroversion.

## Evaluation criteria

Self/leadership signals:

- Recharge rituals protected
- Async preference when calls aren’t needed
- Visible then clean exit at events
- Depth over noise in meetings
- Open introvert identity without apology

## Common candidate mistakes

- Forcing extrovert game 24/7 and burning out
- Believing quiet = not credible
- Trying to “fix” introversion / pretend to be something else
- Staying at events past energy without exit strategy

## Follow-up questions

- “Where do I recharge after meetings?”
- “Can this call be async TLDR?”
- “Have I been seen, then can I French-exit?”
- “Am I leading with depth or competing on volume?”

## Answer frameworks

### Introvert workplace playbook

1. Block recharge time after dense meetings  
2. Redirect surprise calls → async TLDR  
3. Events: appear → connect → French exit  
4. Meetings: observe, deep questions, listen; speak with care  
5. Name introversion kindly when leaving early  

## Good vs bad examples

### Extrovert game vs return to water

**Drain (verbatim):**

> we force ourselves to stay connected 24-7, playing an extroverts game… Why does it drain me instead? We are fishes jumping out of the water and dreaming of breathing air like the birds

**Shift (verbatim):**

> after back-to-back meetings, I would just block off 30 minutes to reflect… hey, let's hop on a call because it's faster. I protect my space by asking, is there a TLDR version that we can discuss async to protect our calendars?… I unapologically make my French exit… No goodbyes.

**Meeting power (verbatim):**

> I don't need to be loud to lead. Introverts bring depth, not noise. We observe what others miss. We ask deep questions that make others think. We listen with intent… When you speak quietly, the whole room becomes quiet because you're the kind of person that people listen to.

**Identity (verbatim):**

> hey, I'm an introvert. So if I disappear early, it's not you, it's me… You are not a broken bird. You're a fish out of water… return to your ocean is the moment that you can breathe again.

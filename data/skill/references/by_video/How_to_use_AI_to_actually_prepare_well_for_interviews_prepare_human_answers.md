## Core principles

- AI won’t magically get you “300% faster + 7-figure offer” — master **natural intelligence** first.
- Most people use AI like a better Google → **same generic answers** everyone else gets.
- Real masters: match **your story to the company’s story**; show **curiosity + self-awareness** (not performed confidence); sound **real**, not “native English.”
- Best candidates in the AI age are **part machine, part human** — machine organizes, human connects.
- Yesterday: smartest had the best answers. Today: smartest **ask the best questions to AI**.
- Be half machine, half human, **100% unforgettable**.

## Interview philosophy

Generic GPT resume/intro/answers are table stakes and no longer differentiate. Differentiation = JD-deep understanding + selected core competencies + custom narrative that could **only** be you for **this** company/role (demo: DoorDash-specific intro).

## Evaluation criteria

- AI output personalized beyond dry achievement list
- Answer could only come from you + only fit this role/company
- Human judgment still selects which competency to lead with
- Questions to AI > copying answers from AI

## Common candidate mistakes

- Believing AI alone lands elite offers.
- GPT resume = “perfect”; reading AI answers in interviews = “genius”; DeepSeek intro = “interesting.”
- Using AI as search → generic scripts.
- Performing confidence instead of curiosity/self-awareness.
- Trying to sound like a native speaker instead of real.

## Follow-up questions

Process checks:

- “Could this intro only be me for this company?”
- “What question did I ask AI that others aren’t asking?”
- “Did I pick the competency, or did I accept the first dump?”

## Answer frameworks

### Interview-copilot pipeline (demo)

1. Don’t stop at “write my self-intro from resume” (good but common).
2. **Analyze the JD** with a detailed teardown prompt → plain English (prompt offered in comments, not in transcript).
3. Ask AI to identify **core competencies** that help you stand out; **you** select which to use.
4. Combine JD understanding + chosen competencies → **custom self-intro** (only-you / only-this-role).

## Good vs bad examples

### Level-1 GPT intro from resume

**OK years ago:** Personalized from resume — “pretty good”; five/three years ago would be strong.

**Problem now:** Everyone and their grandmother can do this → doesn’t stand out.

### Level-2 JD + competency + combine

**Better:** After JD teardown + competency selection + combine → custom intro “clearly made with care” — could only come from you for DoorDash in that specific role — already better before coach edits.

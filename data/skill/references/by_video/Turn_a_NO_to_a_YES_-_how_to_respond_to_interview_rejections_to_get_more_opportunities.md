## Core principles

- After rejection, a **right email** can open the next door; one email can close a door.
- Skip **no-reply** addresses — find recruiter/HM email or LinkedIn.
- Send rejection follow-up within **24 hours**.
- Don’t only apply quietly later — **reply on the same thread** when a new opening appears.
- Humans hire humans they **trust and have a relationship with**.

## Interview philosophy

Rejection isn’t always final. Graceful follow-up builds goodwill; later check-ins make you the easiest “yes” among cold resumes because they already liked you enough to interview you.

## Evaluation criteria

(For follow-up quality)

- Real human recipient (not no-reply)
- Within 24 hours; gracious + stay interested + ask to check in later
- Later: reply to thread, name new role, optional growth update, offer updated materials

## Common candidate mistakes

- Emailing no-reply addresses.
- Doing nothing after rejection.
- Believing “this will never work / don’t bother HR.”
- When a new role opens: quietly apply and hope, without leveraging the prior relationship.

## Answer frameworks

1. Rejection response (≤24h) → thank, disappointed but respectful, remain interested, ask to check in in a few months.
2. New opening → reply on same thread: still interested, optional update, pick conversation back up, offer materials.

## Jonathan's sample answers (verbatim)

### Rejection follow-up email (≤24h)

- **Central question this Short addresses:** Full system after rejection — response email + later new-opening follow-up.
- **Interview question(s) this sample answers:** Post-rejection outreach (email scripts).
- **Jonathan's sample answer (verbatim):**

> Hello interviewer. Thank you for getting back to me. Although I'm disappointed to not be moving forward, I sincerely enjoyed our conversations and appreciated the transparency throughout the process. It gave me an even deeper respect for the team and what you're building at company. I remain highly interested in working here someday. I'd love to stay connected. Would it be okay if I checked in again in a few months? If a future role aligns, I'd be excited to be considered again, especially given our positive conversations this time around. Thank you again, your name.

### New opening follow-up (reply to thread)

- **Jonathan's sample answer (verbatim):**

> Hi interviewer. Hope you've been well. I wanted to quickly check in as I noticed new openings on the team specifically for this job title. I'm still extremely interested in contributing to company. Optional here, you can share a quick update about your career, job search, as long as it shows professional growth, of course. I would love to pick up our conversation back up if the timing works. Happy to share my updated materials if helpful. Warmly, your name.

- Note: “Optional here…” is Jonathan’s instruction inside the script — keep as TEMPLATE guidance when coaching.

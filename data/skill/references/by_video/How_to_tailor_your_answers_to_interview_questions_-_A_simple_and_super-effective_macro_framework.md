## Core principles

- “Right fit” = matching the company’s **season**, not a vague vibe.
- Four seasons: **Spring** (builders), **Summer** (scalers), **Fall** (stewards), **Winter** (healers).
- Dress your language for the season — use/avoid season-specific keywords.
- Seasons apply to **company, org, and team** independently (can differ at once).

## Interview philosophy

Companies (and teams) want different personas by growth stage. Keyword mismatch (e.g. “stabilize” at a spring startup, “build from scratch” at fall) signals wrong fit. Diagnose season before answering.

## Evaluation criteria

- Correct season diagnosis
- Keywords aligned to that season
- Avoided anti-keywords for that season
- Awareness that company/org/team seasons may diverge

## Common candidate mistakes

- Treating “right fit” as mysterious / always “not you.”
- Using preserve/stabilize language at spring companies that need survival growth.
- Using optimize/refine at summer hypergrowth (they want double, not 1%).
- Using build-from-scratch / play-to-win at fall harvest companies.
- Using take-risk / move-fast at winter turnarounds (already afraid).

## Follow-up questions

Before the interview: “What season are they in right now?”

## Answer frameworks

### Four Seasons keyword map

| Season | Who they need | Use | Avoid |
|--------|---------------|-----|-------|
| Spring (seed/startup) | Builders | build from scratch, zero to one, take risks, resourceful, high ownership, wear many hats | preserve quality, stabilize |
| Summer (hypergrowth) | Scalers | scale, establish process, move fast, play to win | optimize, refine |
| Fall (harvest/sustain) | Stewards | refine, optimize, maintain excellence, mentor, preserve quality, raise the bar, long-term value | build from scratch, play to win |
| Winter (turnaround) | Healers | influence without authority, rebuild, stabilize, simplify, focus, build trust | take risk, move fast |

## Core principles

- 面试日志（interview journal）是把“连续被拒”转成“可学习反馈循环”的工具。
- 他说自己曾连挂 11 场，靠日志才从“you were great but…”走到 Google/Uber offer。
- 方法是每轮面试后写两栏：
  1. **Past reflection**（这一轮发生了什么）
  2. **Future focus**（下一轮要改什么、保留什么、相信什么）
- 日志的价值：每个 no 都变成 yes 之前的增量，拒绝不再是纯情绪，而是数据。

## Interview philosophy

如果你不从自己身上学习，就等于没在学习。面试成长依赖复盘质量，而不是运气或记忆。

## Evaluation criteria

- 是否每轮都记录：round/company/interviewer/type
- 是否记录：what went well / what tripped me up / emotional state
- 是否明确下一步：one thing improve / one thing keep / one belief to try
- 是否保留给 future self 的提醒与鼓励

## Common candidate mistakes

- 被拒后只焦虑，不记录
- 复盘只写“没发挥好”，没有具体问题/行为/情绪
- 没有把“下一轮具体动作”写成一句可执行话术

## Follow-up questions

- 我这轮面试最具体的失误点是什么（题型、节奏、表达）？
- 下一轮我只改哪一个动作？
- 我要继续保持什么？
- 我下轮的新信念是什么（例如 I belong here / I don’t need to be perfect）？

## Answer frameworks

### Two-column journal template

**Column 1 (Past)**  
- What round was this?  
- What went well?  
- What tripped me up?  
- How did I feel before/during/after?

**Column 2 (Future)**  
- One thing to improve  
- One thing to keep doing  
- One new belief to practice  
- Note to future self

## Good vs bad examples

### 反应式焦虑 vs 结构化复盘（verbatim）

> if you're not learning from yourself, then you are not learning period.

### 候选人复盘实例（verbatim snippets）

> What went well? I used all three stories that I prepared.

> One thing to improve, pause to clarify the question before jumping in. Next time I'll ask, just to clarify, are you asking more about X or Y?

> New thought to practice. If I asked for clarification, I am not dumb. I am a clear communicator.

> This round wasn't perfect, but I am also closer to my offer because this was my best interview yet.

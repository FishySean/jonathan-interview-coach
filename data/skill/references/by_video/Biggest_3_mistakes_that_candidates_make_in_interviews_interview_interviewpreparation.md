## Core principles

- Three failure archetypes every Chinese professional interviewing in the West should avoid: **Matt GPT**, **the reporter**, **the over-explainer**.
- In the age of perfect AI answers, when everyone sounds smart, **smart stops mattering — be human**.
- **Facts inform, stories persuade** — Western interviewers demand the shift from facts to stories (why behavioral interviews are common).
- Finishing a project is not an achievement — it’s **doing your job**.
- Confidence isn’t always saying more — it’s knowing **when you’ve said enough**.
- Don’t perform — **connect**. (Matt GPT → perfection; reporter → facts; over-explainer → approval.)

## Interview philosophy

Hiring managers (e.g. building Shopify team 0→100) lose trust when answers sound recited/generic, get dry reports instead of impact stories, or drown in TMI that signals low confidence.

## Evaluation criteria

- Specific to *your* career + *this* company (not swappable company-name answers)
- Sounds human, not memorized 人机合作面试
- Proudest achievement = impact on team/business/customer via story, not event list
- Tell-me-about-yourself = relevant, concise — not 10-year irrelevant saga / CEO-video flex / layoff dump

## Common candidate mistakes

1. **Matt GPT:** Perfect, fluent, textbook; “why leave” = generic growth/culture/values; join-us reasons work for any company.
2. **The reporter:** Amazing resume (Ivy, big company, title) but proudest achievement = dry events/facts/numbers; even after probe, “I finished the project and the customers were happy.”
3. **The over-explainer:** Tell-me-about-yourself from 10 years ago in irrelevant industry; CEO videos watched; layoff mention; too much info → interviewer more concerned.

## Follow-up questions

Jonathan’s probe to the reporter:

- “What impact did you have on your team, the business, your customer?”

Coach reframes:

- “Could this answer only come from me and only fit this company?”
- “Am I reporting facts or telling a persuasive story?”
- “Have I said enough?”

## Answer frameworks

| Archetype | Depends on | Fix |
|-----------|------------|-----|
| Matt GPT | Perfection / AI-perfect scripts | Customize — only-you + only-this-company |
| Reporter | Facts / credentials | Shift facts → stories; show business impact |
| Over-explainer | Approval via volume | Edit ruthlessly; confidence = enough, not more |

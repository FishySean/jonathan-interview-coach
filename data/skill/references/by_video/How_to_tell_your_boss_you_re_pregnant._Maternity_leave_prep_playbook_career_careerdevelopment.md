## Core principles

- Managers want to celebrate — but must think about **workload, stakeholders, teammates**.
- Handled poorly, the conversation can permanently hurt the relationship even after return.
- Use **Acknowledge, Assure, Affirm, and Align** so you, team, and manager succeed while you’re out.
- Bring a **draft high-level transition plan** early; propose regular touch points; plan to step back in with momentum.

## Interview philosophy

Not an interview Short — career conversation design: personal news + operational empathy + partnership framing.

## Evaluation criteria

Good disclosure:

- Exciting personal framing (not apology dump)
- Acknowledge team adjustments
- Assure continuity with a drafted plan
- Affirm appreciation for manager support
- Align via regular touch points for return

## Common candidate mistakes

**Bad opens (verbatim):**

> Uh, so just letting you know, I'm pregnant. So I guess I need to take maternity leave soon?

> Yes, I'm informing you that I will be taking maternity leave effective two weeks from now, wishing you and the team all the best.

- No transition plan / no partnership ask
- Cold “informing you” with abrupt effective date

## Follow-up questions

- “Have I drafted a high-level handoff plan?”
- “Did I acknowledge impact and ask to partner on transition?”
- “Did I affirm the manager and propose touch points for return?”

## Answer frameworks

### Acknowledge → Assure → Affirm → Align

1. **Acknowledge** — exciting news; may mean team adjustments; want to make transition smooth
2. **Assure** — drafted high-level transition / handoffs for continuity
3. **Affirm** — appreciate support/guidance
4. **Align** — regular touch points; return and keep momentum

## Core principles

- Hesitation comes from making **success the goal** — a toxic definition that drains you.
- Free people chase **process**; success is an inevitability when small things are done right.
- Tying self-worth to uncontrollable outcomes makes every risk feel life-or-death.
- Reframe: present to collect criticism; volunteer to see how you react; **interview to earn a rejection that shows where to improve.**
- Embrace the worst → worst can’t happen; every failure eliminates a wrong path.
- Provocation: What if the goal wasn’t to succeed — what would you say to fail badly and learn fastest?

## Interview philosophy

Interview performance anxiety is identity threat. Detach worth from offer outcomes; treat interviews as learning loops.

## Evaluation criteria

Mindset signals (self-coaching):

- Process over outcome
- Rejection as data
- Risk without identity collapse

## Common candidate mistakes

- Chasing success as the goal → freeze in meetings/interviews
- Rehearse then can’t speak when time comes
- Treating failure as identity threat

## Follow-up questions

- “Am I interviewing for the offer, or for the learning rejection?”
- “What uncontrollable outcome have I tied my worth to?”
- “What would I attempt if failing badly were the goal?”

## Answer frameworks

### Practice loop

1. Do the scary thing for feedback, not applause  
2. Volunteer for stretch to observe your reaction  
3. Interview to map gaps from rejection  
4. Eliminate paths → find true path  

## Good vs bad examples

### Success-as-identity vs process

**Freeze scene (verbatim):**

> Remember the last time that you went to speak up in a team meeting you even rehearsed it in your head But when your time came You just couldn't speak up… you've changed your self-worth to an outcome that you Cannot control… failure doesn't just sting it threatens your identity

**Reframe (verbatim):**

> Interview for a role not to get the offer but to earn a rejection that tells you Exactly where you can do better next time… When you embrace the worst the worst can't happen anymore because every failure is a learning opportunity

**Open provocation (verbatim):**

> Work so that you may quit interview so that you may fail The reason that you hesitate in everything is because you've made success your goal Let it go
